# Imagem automotiva do login

Gerada em 02/11/2026 com a ferramenta integrada imagegen, modo generate, sem imagens de referência. Arquivo da aplicação: `console-app/public/images/sign-in-car.png`. Substitui a imagem de realidade virtual por solicitação do usuário.

Prompt final:

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: portrait hero photograph for the left panel of the Verifica Placa vehicle information business dashboard login page. Primary request: an elegant realistic modern unbranded graphite blue sedan, front three-quarter angle, parked in a clean contemporary Brazilian urban setting at blue hour. Premium automotive editorial photography, physically realistic bodywork, clean headlights and subtle cool blue reflections, muted navy and slate palette. Composition portrait 1024x1536: car fully readable across the central middle third, camera at headlight level, car front and number plate visible but blank with no legible characters, plenty of dark quiet space at top for an existing brand overlay and bottom 30 percent for existing white title overlay. Subtle architectural background, uncluttered, realistic asphalt, no people. Car should dominate image yet remain fully within central crop. No text, no typography, no logos, no graphic overlays, no virtual reality, no futuristic holograms.

Validada na composição desktop com os textos e a marca sobrepostos em HTML. Imagem decorativa, oculta no layout móvel.
